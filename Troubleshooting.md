# Troubleshooting Common Bluetooth Earbuds Issues
> **Audience:** Everyday End-Users | **Doc Type:** Troubleshooting Guide | **Tools:** Markdown

## Overview
This guide helps quickly resolve most common connectivity and audio issues while using Bluetooth headsets/earphones connected via laptop or phone. 

This guide will cover three most common problems and solutions associated with them.

## 1. Earbuds Not Appearing in Bluetooth Scan
* **Symptom:** Bluetooth not appearing on scanned devices in laptop or mobile
* **Cause:** Earbuds may be switched off or not in pairing mode
* **Resolution:**
 * Make sure earbuds are switched on.
 * Place the earbuds back into their charging case and ensure both the case and earbuds have enough battery.

## 2. Connected, But Audio Still Plays From Device Speakers
* **Symptom:** Bluetooth earbuds are connected but audio plays from device's speakers.
* **Cause:** Bluetooth is not connected to proper device.
* **Resolution:** 
 * Make sure Bluetooth is switched on and connected to appropriate device
 * Switch off the Bluetooth and scan again devices and connect

## 3. Audio Cutting Out or Sounding Choppy
* **Symptom:** Bluetooth earbuds audio is not proper and is sounding choppy
* **Cause:** Bluetooth earbuds battery may be low or they may be far away from connected device
* **Resolution:** 
 * Make sure Bluetooth earbuds have battery 
 * Move yourself to close proximity of connected device.
