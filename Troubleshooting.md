# Troubleshooting Common Bluetooth Earbuds Issues
> **Audience:** Everyday End-Users | **Doc Type:** Troubleshooting Guide | **Tools:** Markdown

## Overview
This guide helps quickly resolve most common connectivity and audio issues while using Bluetooth headsets/earphones connected via laptop or phone. 

> This guide will cover three most common problems and solutions associated with them.

## 1. Earbuds Not Appearing in Bluetooth Scan
* **Symptom:** Bluetooth not appearing on scanned devices in laptop or mobile
* **Cause:** Earbuds may be switched off or not in pairing mode
* **Resolution:**
 1. Make sure earbuds are switched on.
 2. Place the earbuds back into their charging case and ensure both the case and earbuds have enough battery.
